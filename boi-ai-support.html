AI-DRIVEN DECISION SUPPORT SYSTEM FOR INVESTMENT EVALUATION, MONITORING AND FORECASTING
IN SRI LANKA

Intelligence-driven decision support platform for investment proposal evaluation, predictive analytics, and strategic forecasting.

Conducted in collaboration with the Board of Investment (BOI) of Sri Lanka, this project enhances investment evaluation through AI-powered document understanding, predictive credibility scoring, and trend forecasting models, reducing manual bottlenecks and enabling data-driven strategic decisions.


SLIIT Research Project 2025/2026 · Group 25–26J-116
Research Domain

Understanding the Problem Space

Literature Survey

State of the Art in AI-Driven Investment Evaluation

The global investment environment is rapidly evolving, pushing Investment Promotion Agencies (IPAs) to adopt data-driven decision support systems. Advances in NLP have significantly improved how organizations manage large volumes of unstructured legal, regulatory, and financial documents.

Machine Learning has become pivotal in predictive analytics and economic forecasting. Ensemble learning and Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) outperform traditional time-series models on complex, non-linear patterns. Real-time sentiment analysis from public news further enriches credibility and volatility assessments.

Despite these advances, most technologies remain siloed. This gap creates an opportunity for a unified, hierarchical framework supporting the full investment lifecycle from initial proposal assessment to long-term impact forecasting.

Research Gap

What the Current Landscape is Missing

Existing systems treat intelligence gathering, economic forecasting, and risk assessment as separate, disconnected tasks. No widely-adopted multi-module architecture directly links qualitative proposal insights with measurable outcomes like employment generation and FDI growth.

External signals: global news sentiment, market perception, emerging trends are frequently overlooked or analyzed in isolation. Without a centralized visualization dashboard integrating these signals, decision-makers lack a holistic view of investment feasibility and external market conditions.

Research Problem

Operational Bottlenecks at the Board of Investment

The BOI is the governing body for local and foreign investment in Sri Lanka. It faces significant bottlenecks from manually evaluating high volumes of diverse investment proposals. This leads to delayed decisions, inconsistent risk assessments, and an inability to accurately forecast long-term economic contributions.

Without automated investor credibility verification correlated with real-time global trends, the organization remains vulnerable to human error and information gaps in a rapidly changing global economy.

Research Objectives

Five Pillars of the System

01

Automated Intelligence

Establish a document processing module powered by hierarchical RAG to support efficient proposal summarization and semantic question-answering.

02

Predictive Assessment

Design ML models for evaluating investor credibility, estimating future investment amount, and forecasting potential job creation outcomes.

03

Economic Forecasting

Generate quarterly FDI forecasts through a dedicated forecasting module built on historical investment data and time-series analysis.

04

Trend Integration

Introduce a sector trend prediction module combining GNNs with sentiment analysis to capture cross-sector investment movements and emerging market trends.

05

Centralized Visualization

Consolidate all analytical module outputs into an interactive dashboard enabling streamlined stakeholder decision-making across the investment lifecycle.

Methodology

How the System Works

The system is an integrated decision-support application that streamlines investment proposal evaluation through multiple intelligent modules. The process begins with document upload, followed by automatic extraction of key information for analysis of both textual content and supporting metadata.

Stage 01

Document Ingestion

Investment proposal documents are uploaded and processed by the hierarchical RAG pipeline for intelligent information extraction.

Stage 02

Investor Credibility

ML models analyze investor-related information to determine trustworthiness, reliability, and credibility indicators.

Stage 03

Project Feasibility

The proposal is evaluated for viability,investment amount and estimated job creation outcomes.

Stage 04

Sector Intelligence

Public news sentiment and cross-sector relationship patterns are gathered and analyzed using GNN-based trend detection.

Stage 05

FDI Forecasting

Historical investment data drives quarterly FDI forecast generation with explanatory insights supporting predicted trends.
